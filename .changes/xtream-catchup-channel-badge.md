Replay-capable Xtream channels show a non-interactive Catchup badge in the
live list. Android TV catch-up stays beside the EPG; OK enables slider seeking
without opening the keyboard, BACK exits, and holding LEFT/RIGHT accelerates.
A quick double OK on a replay programme opens it fullscreen.
Android XMLTV schedules now include the preceding 24 hours for catch-up.
